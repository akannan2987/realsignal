How many communities could you build?

Pick how many strains you have, how many go into each community, and how many communities a laboratory can test in a year. The page does the arithmetic: n choose k, the number of different ways to pick k strains out of n when the order doesn't matter.

An assumption you choose: the slider moves in growing steps from 10 to 10,000 a year. For scale, each round of the published screen fitted 80 sealed boxes into one growth chamber, and the plants grew for about five and a half weeks.
